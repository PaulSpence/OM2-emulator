## Paper framing (agreed 2026-11-02)

- Main text: three emulators, all with the hard global budget constraint:
  ConvNeXt U-Net (Samudra architecture, run name `samudra`), deterministic UNet (`deterministic`), UNet + CRPS ensemble (`crps_ensemble`).
  Call it "ConvNeXt U-Net (Samudra architecture)", not "Samudra".
- One figure on the constraint itself: without it the forced heat uptake is missed by 62%, with it 0.1%
  (`samudra_style`, labelled "without budget closure").

- Forcing is flux forcing (JRA55-do-driven ACCESS-OM2 fluxes), as in Samudra. Coupled/ZECMIP is future work; FAFMIP is a better fit for this version.
- Stability test: 1,000-year balanced RYF control (May 1990 – April 1991 repeated, net global heat and freshwater flux removed from both the network input and the constraint).

### 1,000-year balanced RYF control: `figures/control_ryf_1000yr/`, data in `runs/control_ryf_1000yr/<name>.nc`

| Emulator | max abs z > 20 from | NaN from |
|---|---|---|
| samudra (ConvNeXt) | year 14 | year 68 |
| deterministic UNet | year 13 | year 424 |
| crps_ensemble (UNet + CRPS) | year 12 | year 345 |
| diffusion, diffusion_onestep | year 5 | years 264–342 |
| samudra_crps (ConvNeXt + CRPS) | never (max ~6) | – |
| samudra_style (no constraint) | never (max ~5) | – |

Interpretation: after years 10–15 the growth is exponential (straight line on a log plot; e-folding ~5–6 yr for the deterministic UNet), i.e. an unstable mode of the learned monthly map. The global constraint cannot stop it (it fixes only the global total), which is why the earlier 50-year control plots (global totals only) looked fine. Not a forcing artifact: two emulators ran the same forcing and code for 1,000 years stably. The 14-year skill test ends just as the growth appears, so skill scores did not catch it.

This undercuts the stability claim for the UNet emulators. ConvNeXt + CRPS is currently the only constrained emulator stable for 1,000 years.
